# Reality Pilot 002 — consolidated evidence request

Status: `REALITY_PILOT_002_BLOCKED_WITH_EVIDENCE_REQUEST`

Pilot 002 needs one further real, source-grounded executed case in addition to
ONE-N524 and ONE-N617. Please supply one coherent case package. Cape Town is
preferred if its original operational thread is available; otherwise another
completed One-Way case with a distinct contract/booking identifier is suitable.

## Minimum required material

1. A source that binds the case identity: contract/booking reference, equipment
   quantity and type, origin, and authorized redelivery location.
2. A release, pick-up, gate-out, or equivalent execution record tied to that
   identity or to named unit numbers.
3. A movement, arrival, gate-in, off-hire, or return record tied to the same
   identity or named unit numbers. At least three source-grounded business
   events and two valid transitions must be supportable across the case.
4. For a completed-case claim, a depot EIR, owner confirmation, or other
   primary return/off-hire record with a date. A request for off-hire details is
   not confirmation.

## Fields that must remain

- contract and booking references;
- container number(s), or a stable lot identifier plus quantity/type;
- event type and event date exactly as recorded;
- origin, destination, redelivery/depot identity;
- document date, sender/issuer role, and a stable document locator;
- enough document content to recompute a file hash and trace each assertion.

## Fields that may be redacted

- bank account and payment-routing details;
- personal phone numbers, signatures, and personal email addresses;
- commercial amounts, where they are not necessary to establish the event;
- unrelated container rows and unrelated message recipients.

Redaction must retain the document structure, the relevant row/page/message
locator, and a record that the supplied copy is redacted.

## Optional material

- signed contract;
- payment allocation and payment confirmation;
- per-container EIRs for every unit;
- damage and dispute correspondence.

Payment evidence is optional for Pilot 002 admission. Without it, no `SETTLED`
or `CLOSED` claim will be made.
